Make a computer reachable from your other devices when you are not on its network.

## Applicability

There are two ways. **Account access** links the computer to your Zuse account; any device signed in to that account can find it. **Tailscale** offers the computer's tailnet address to devices you pair; it needs no Zuse account. Neither copies your repositories anywhere or installs agents.

## Prerequisites

- The desktop app or `zuse serve` is running on the computer.
- For account access: a Zuse account, and a terminal on the computer.
- For Tailscale: Tailscale (or a Headscale client) signed in on the computer and on your other devices.

## Steps

### With your account

1. In a terminal on the computer, run:

   ```sh
   zuse link --name studio-mac
   ```

2. If Zuse prints an address and a code, open the address on any device and confirm the code.
3. Wait for `Available through your account as studio-mac at …`.

On a headless computer you can instead start Serve with `zuse serve --account`.

To open the computer:

- **Desktop app on another computer:** sign in, open **Settings → Remote access**, and choose **Connect** next to the computer under **Devices**.
- **Mobile app:** open **Settings**, choose **Sign in to Zuse**, and sign in with the same account.

### With Tailscale

1. On the computer, open **Settings → Remote access** and turn on **Tailscale**, or start `zuse serve --lan`.
2. Pair each device once with **Connect device** (see [Connect a phone](/how-to/remote-connect-device/)). Paired devices keep using the tailnet when they leave your network.

## Verification

- `zuse status` on the computer shows the signed-in account and `Computer: <name> at <address>` for account access, or a Tailscale route.
- From the other device, the computer's projects and chats load and an existing chat opens.

## Troubleshooting and recovery

### The computer does not appear on the other device

Confirm both use the same account and that `zuse status` shows the computer as linked. Run `zuse link` again if it was unlinked.

### The computer shows "Update Zuse on this computer to connect."

Update Zuse on the linked computer, then connect again.

### You no longer want account access

Run `zuse unlink` on the computer. Local chats stay, and devices paired over Wi-Fi or Tailscale keep working.
