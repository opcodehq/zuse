Pair the Zuse app on your phone with the computer that owns your projects. This does not need a Zuse account.

## Applicability

Use this guide when the phone can reach the computer over the same Wi-Fi or your tailnet. To reach it from anywhere with your account, see [Reach a computer away from its network](/how-to/remote-hosted-access/). The computer runs the agents in both cases.

## Prerequisites

- Zuse is open on the computer, or `zuse serve` is running there.
- The Zuse app is on your phone.
- Both devices are on the same network, or both are on your tailnet.

## Steps

1. On the computer, open **Settings → Remote access**.
2. Under **This computer**, turn on **Wi-Fi**, **Tailscale**, or both. The status changes to **Reachable on your Wi-Fi** or **Reachable at home and away**.
3. Choose **Connect device**. The **Pair a device** dialog shows a QR code on the **Phone** tab.
4. On the phone, tap **Add computer** and scan the code. If the camera is not available, choose **Copy link** on the computer and paste it under **Or paste a link**.
5. Wait for the dialog to say the device is connected, then close it.

On a headless computer, run `zuse serve --lan`, then `zuse pair --app` to show the QR code in the terminal.

## Verification

- The computer's projects and chats appear on the phone.
- The phone is listed under **Devices** in **Settings → Remote access**, with the date it paired.
- An existing chat opens and shows its latest messages.

## Troubleshooting and recovery

### Connect device is unavailable

No connection is on. Turn on **Wi-Fi** or **Tailscale** first.

### The code expired

Codes last ten minutes and work once. Choose **New code**.

### The phone cannot reach the computer

Guest and office Wi-Fi networks often block devices from talking to each other. Use Tailscale, or pair over SSH with **Add computer → Connect with SSH** ([details](/remote/nearby-pairing/#pair-over-ssh)).

### The phone was revoked

Retrying cannot restore a revoked pairing. Forget the computer on the phone and pair again with a new code.

See [connection recovery](/how-to/remote-recover-connection/) for later disconnects.
