Antigravity runs through Google's ACP server (`agy_acp_server`). Zuse installs it for you: there's no shell command to run.

## Install and sign in

1. Open **Settings → Providers & accounts**. Until it's installed, the row reads **Install Antigravity to enable**.
2. Choose **Install**. Zuse downloads Google's release archive for your OS and CPU and verifies it against a pinned digest, the ACP registry's digest, or Google's code signature.
3. Sign in from the row with your Google account. To use a different Google account, remove the connected account, then connect again.

Builds are available for macOS, Linux, and Windows on x86_64 and ARM64. Zuse checks the ACP registry for newer releases.

## Workflows in Zuse

- Choose from Antigravity's models. Reasoning effort is part of each model rather than a separate control.
- Messages you send during a turn are delivered when the turn ends.
- Discover skills from `.gemini/skills`, `.agents/skills`, and Antigravity's own skills folders.
- Use Plan mode, **Ask for approval**, and **Full access**.
