Zuse runs Oh My Pi through your installed `omp` CLI over ACP (`omp acp`). It's separate from the [Pi](/providers/pi) integration.

## Install and sign in

```bash
curl -fsSL https://omp.sh/install | sh
omp login
```

Zuse looks for `omp` on your PATH, in `~/.local/bin`, in mise's shims, and in `/opt/homebrew/bin` and `/usr/local/bin`. Set `OMP_EXECUTABLE` to use another installation. Update with `omp update`, or from the Oh My Pi row.

## Workflows in Zuse

- Choose from the models Oh My Pi offers in the session.
- Choose a thinking level from Minimal to XHigh.
- Steer a running turn; your message is delivered mid-turn.
- Discover skills from `.agents/skills`.
- Use Plan mode, **Ask for approval**, and **Full access**.
