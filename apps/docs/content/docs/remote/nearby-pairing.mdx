Pairing gives the Zuse app on your phone its own access to one computer. It does not need a Zuse account. The phone keeps the pairing and reconnects on its own until you revoke it.

<Workflow>
  <Step title="Let the phone reach the computer">
    In the desktop app, open **Settings → Remote access** and turn on **Wi-Fi**, **Tailscale**, or both. The status under **This computer** changes from **Only this computer can use Zuse** to **Reachable on your Wi-Fi** or **Reachable at home and away**. On a headless computer, run `zuse serve --lan`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show a code">
    Choose **Connect device**. The **Pair a device** dialog opens on the **Phone** tab with a QR code and a countdown. On a headless computer, run `zuse pair --app` to print the QR code in the terminal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan it">
    In the Zuse app, tap **Add computer** and point the camera at the code. If the phone cannot scan, choose **Copy link** in the dialog and paste it under **Or paste a link** on the phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The dialog shows that the device is connected, and the phone appears under **Devices**.
  </Step>
</Workflow>

A code works once and expires after ten minutes. Choose **New code** for another one; an unused code is discarded when you replace it.

## Pair over SSH

If the computer is not set up yet, or only listens on loopback, the phone can do it over SSH. In the app, choose **Add computer → Connect with SSH** and enter `user@host` (`user@host:2222` and `ssh://user@host` also work). The phone creates its own SSH key. Add that key to the computer, or enter your password once so Zuse can add it to `~/.ssh/authorized_keys`; the password is not saved.

The phone then runs `zuse pair` on the computer. If Zuse is not running there, it offers to start `zuse serve`, and **Keep running after restart** installs it with `zuse daemon install --serve`. The computer must already have the `zuse` command. The SSH host key is trusted the first time, and a changed key is refused. When the computer only accepts local connections, the phone reaches it through the SSH session.

## Revoke a phone

On the computer, open **Settings → Remote access**, find the phone under **Devices**, and choose **Revoke**. This ends its access at once. Removing the computer on the phone (**Forget computer**) only deletes the phone's copy.
