Remote access lets another device use a computer that is already running Zuse. That computer owns the repositories, agents, credentials, terminals, and chats. A phone, browser, or other desktop app sends requests to it and shows the results; nothing moves onto the other device.

## Where to find it

In the desktop app, open **Settings → Remote access**. The page has three parts:

- **This computer** shows whether other devices can reach it, the **Connect device** button, and a switch for each way to connect: **Wi-Fi** and **Tailscale**. **Zuse Relay** is listed as coming soon.
- **Devices** lists every paired phone, browser, and computer with a **Revoke** button, plus computers linked to your Zuse account. The **+** button adds another computer to this app.
- **Advanced settings** holds browser access at an address and certificate you choose, Tailscale HTTPS, and the device registry for your workspace.

On a computer without a display, the same options are flags on [`zuse serve`](/serve/).

## Ways to connect

| Way | Reaches the computer from | Turn it on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wi-Fi** | Devices on the same network | The **Wi-Fi** switch, or `zuse serve --lan` |
| **Tailscale** | Devices on your tailnet, at home or away. Headscale works too. | The **Tailscale** switch, or `zuse serve --lan` |
| **Zuse account** | Devices signed in to your account, from any network | `zuse link`, or `zuse serve --account` |

Zuse picks the fastest working route on its own and moves to a better one when it appears, for example from Tailscale to Wi-Fi when you get home. Every route is end-to-end encrypted between the device and the computer.

<SecurityNote title="Execution stays on the computer">
Remote access does not install agents on the other device. Agent CLIs and their sign-ins must exist on the computer that runs Zuse.
</SecurityNote>
