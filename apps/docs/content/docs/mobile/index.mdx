The Zuse mobile apps for iPhone and Android are clients for your computers and for Zuse Cloud. Projects, agents, and chats stay on the computer you connect to; the phone shows them and sends your messages and decisions.

<Callout title="Availability">
Zuse's desktop releases on GitHub do not include the mobile apps. The steps in this section describe the apps as they exist in the Zuse source.
</Callout>

## What you can do

- See chats from all your computers in one list, search them, and archive or restore them.
- Start a chat on a computer or in Zuse Cloud, in a new worktree, the main checkout, an existing branch, or an existing worktree.
- Choose the agent and how much it may do without asking, and turn on plan mode.
- Send text, photos, and files, and stop a running response.
- Approve or deny agent requests, answer questions, and approve plans.
- Review working-tree, staged, and branch changes.

## What runs where

The phone does not clone repositories or run agent CLIs. The agents you can pick come from the connected computer, so two computers can offer different agents.

## Connect a computer

On the phone, tap **Add computer**. You can scan a QR code from the computer, paste a link, or sign in to the computer over SSH and let Zuse set it up. See [Connect your phone](/mobile/connect/).
